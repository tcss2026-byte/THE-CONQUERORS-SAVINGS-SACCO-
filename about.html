ABOUT US

The Conquerors Savings SACCO is a family-based savings and financial empowerment organization dedicated to helping members achieve economic growth and financial stability.

We bring together individuals and families with a common goal of cultivating a strong savings culture, promoting responsible financial management and supporting one another in times of need.

Through unity, trust, transparency and commitment, we provide a platform where members can save, grow investments and improve their livelihoods.
