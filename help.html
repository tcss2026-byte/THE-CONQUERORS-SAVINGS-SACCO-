Welcome to the Help Center

Welcome to The Conquerors Savings Sacco Management System. This platform helps members and administrators manage savings, membership applications, transactions and communication efficiently.

1. Creating an Account

To use the system, you must first create an account.

  • Open the Login Page.
  • Click Create Account.
  • Enter First Name.
  • Enter Second Name.
  • Enter Phone Number.
  • Create Username.
  • Create Password.
  • Confirm Password.
  • Click Create Account.

2. User Login

  • Enter Username.
  • Enter Password.
  • Click Login.

After successful login, you will be redirected to the Dashboard.

3. Applying for Membership

Creating an account does not automatically make you a member.

  • Login to your account.
  • Open Dashboard.
  • Click Apply for Membership.
  • Wait for Administrator approval.

4. Membership Approval

Only Administrators can approve or reject membership applications.

  • Review applications.
  • Approve applications.
  • Reject applications.
  • Generate Member IDs.
  • Create Member Accounts.

5. Searching Members

Administrators can search members using:

  • Member Name.
  • Phone Number.
  • Member ID.

6. Member Profiles

Each member profile contains:

  • Profile Picture.
  • Full Name.
  • Member ID.
  • Phone Number.
  • Current Balance.
  • Transaction History.
  • Latest Receipt.

7. Deposits

Administrators can record deposits.

  • Open Member Profile.
  • Enter Amount.
  • Click Deposit.

The balance is automatically updated and a receipt is generated.

8. Withdrawals

Administrators can record withdrawals.

  • Open Member Profile.
  • Enter Amount.
  • Click Withdraw.

Withdrawals cannot exceed the available balance.

9. Transaction History

Every deposit and withdrawal is stored automatically.

Transaction records contain:

  • Transaction Type.
  • Amount.
  • Date and Time.
  • Member ID.

10. Receipts

A receipt is generated after every transaction.

Each receipt contains:

  • Member Name.
  • Member ID.
  • Transaction Type.
  • Amount.
  • Date and Time.

11. Profile Pictures

Administrators can upload and update member profile pictures.

  • Open Member Profile.
  • Select Image.
  • Click Update Photo.

12. Contacting Members

The system allows direct communication through:

  • Phone Calls.
  • SMS Messages.
  • WhatsApp Messages.

13. Deleting Transactions

Only Administrators can delete transactions.

When a transaction is deleted:

  • The transaction record is removed.
  • The member balance is adjusted automatically.

14. Deleting Members

Only Administrators can permanently delete members.

Deleting a member removes:

  • Member Record.
  • User Account.
  • Membership Request.
  • Transaction History.

This action cannot be reversed.

15. Administrator Functions

  • Approve Memberships.
  • Reject Memberships.
  • Manage Members.
  • Make Deposits.
  • Make Withdrawals.
  • Delete Transactions.
  • Delete Members.
  • Update Profile Photos.

16. Security Guidelines

  • Never share your password.
  • Always logout after use.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Keep Administrator credentials confidential.
  • Protect member information.

17. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I become a member immediately after creating an account?

A: No. You must first apply for membership and wait for approval.

Q: Who can make deposits and withdrawals?

A: Only Administrators.

Q: Can deleted transactions be recovered?

A: No. Deleted transactions are permanently removed.

Q: Can I update my profile picture?

A: Yes, through the member profile page.
